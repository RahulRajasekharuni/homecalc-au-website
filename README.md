# HomeCalc AU

### Know what a property will actually cost you.

HomeCalc AU is a Chrome extension for Australian residential property buyers. It adds a practical cost and comparison panel to supported listings on realestate.com.au and Domain.

Instead of looking only at the advertised price, HomeCalc helps owners and investors understand the estimated upfront costs, mortgage repayments, rental performance and personal suitability of a property.

---

## What HomeCalc provides

- Purchase-cost estimates across all Australian states and territories
- Deposit and total cash-required calculations
- Transfer duty, applicable concessions and foreign-buyer surcharges
- Mortgage repayment estimates
- Separate Owner and Investor modes
- Gross yield, net yield and cash-flow estimates for investors
- Separate owner and investor property shortlists
- Personal Fit comparisons based on the user’s priorities
- Editable assumptions when information is missing from a listing
- Table, card and CSV comparison views

Calculations and assumptions are shown clearly so users can review and adjust them.

## How it works

1. Open a supported residential property listing.
2. Open the HomeCalc extension panel.
3. Select Owner or Investor mode.
4. Review and adjust the property, finance and personal-preference inputs.
5. Save suitable properties to the relevant comparison shortlist.

Paid purchases are completed securely through Stripe. HomeCalc AU does not receive or store complete payment-card details.

---

## Subscription cancellation

Buyer Pro subscriptions can be cancelled at any time through the billing-management link inside HomeCalc.

Cancellation prevents future renewals. Paid access continues until the end of the current billing period. Partially used billing periods are not normally prorated, except where required by law.

The House Hunter Pass is a one-time purchase and does not renew automatically.

## Privacy

HomeCalc stores listing analyses, user inputs, preferences, notes and shortlist information to provide its features. Much of this information is stored on the user’s device.

When signed in, account information, plan status, usage counts and synced shortlist information may be stored securely by HomeCalc’s service providers.

Listing pages are read by the extension to produce property analyses. HomeCalc does not sell personal information or collect a general history of websites visited.

Payment information is processed securely by Stripe.

Users can export or remove locally stored HomeCalc data through the extension settings.
